## Description (4000 characters max)

One small routine. One day at a time.

Yogi Focus helps you keep a routine the way yogis have for centuries: at the same hour, in the same place, with the same things, and with a kind word where other apps keep score.

MADE FOR MINDS THAT WANDER
If you find it hard to focus, hard to stick to a routine, or you have never kept a habit going for long, "do this every day for the rest of your life" is too heavy to carry. So Yogi Focus never asks it. There are no streaks to protect, no red badges and no guilt. A missed day is met with "just start".

THREE IDEAS, BORROWED FROM YOGIS
• Only today. Each morning a yogi asks one question: this is a new day, how will I keep my practice today?
• Same time, same place, same things. When nothing around the routine changes, there is nothing to decide and nothing to remember.
• One mandala is 48 days. A practice kept for one mandala is likely to stay.

HOW IT HELPS
• Choose one routine: meditate, stretch, gym, walk, cut sugar, read, journal, play music, study, tidy up, plan your day, or your own.
• Give it a home: the hour, the place and the things you use. Ten minutes is a gentle start; choose up to an hour if your practice needs it.
• Tap the nudge and begin. Your timer opens ready to go, shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and ends with a soft chime.
• On a harder day, choose the two-minute version. It still keeps the day. And if today isn't the day, "Not today" is a kind answer too.
• A Home Screen widget does the remembering. It shows the hour, the place and what to bring, says "It's time" at your hour, and has Done and Not today buttons.
• Apple Watch gives a gentle tap twenty minutes before and again at the hour. Tap Done, Not today, or start your timer, right on your wrist. Prefer something firmer? Turn on a gentle alarm.
• Reminders follow the hour you really practise, and grow lighter as the routine becomes yours.
• Siri, Shortcuts and the Action button: say "I've done my meditation" and the day is kept.
• Keep practice time clear by quieting distracting apps, or turn on Do Not Disturb with one tap.
• Now and then, one kind question: how did it feel? Scattered, steady or absorbed. Every answer is a good one.
• A lotus that blooms with you. Each day you practise opens a petal. Yogi Focus keeps the lotus out of sight for the first 30 days, so every day can simply be today. Then it shows you how far you have come.
• Finish a mandala and share it as a short video of your lotus opening, made right on your phone.

GENTLE BY DESIGN
• The boring middle, from about day 7 to day 27, is normal. In those weeks Yogi Focus has one message for you: just start.
• A missed day never resets anything. Your mandala carries on to 48 days.
• Want a fresh start? Begin a routine again whenever you choose.
• Start with one routine: one at a time is easier to keep. You can add more whenever you like, and Yogi Focus will gently suggest giving each one a full mandala first.

PRIVATE
No account, no tracking, no ads. Your routines stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, so they follow you to a new iPhone. Your mandala video is made on your phone and goes only where you share it. Nothing is sent to us.

FREE FOR EVERYONE, ALWAYS
No subscription, nothing to buy.

For iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch.
