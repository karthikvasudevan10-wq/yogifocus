Three ideas, borrowed from yogis

In India, yogis use the same simple method to master any practice. Yogi Focus is built on it.

Idea one

Only today

“For the rest of your life” is too heavy to carry, so a yogi doesn't carry it. Each morning they ask one question: this is a new day, how will I keep my practice today?

Idea two

Same time, same place, same things

The exact hour, the exact spot, the same mat and the same clothes. When nothing around the routine changes, there is nothing to decide and nothing to remember.

Idea three

One mandala is 48 days

Yogis hold that a practice kept for one mandala, 48 days, starts to show its real benefits and is likely to stay with you for life.

How the 48 days tend to go

Somewhere around day seven the novelty wears off. That stretch is the boring middle, and it is where most routines quietly end.

Questions, answered gently

What if I miss a day, or don't practise on time?

Nothing resets. If the hour slips by, Yogi Focus simply says Still today: just start when you can. If a whole day goes by, the next morning is a new day, with nothing to catch up.

After day 30, when your lotus appears, a missed day shows as a red petal and Yogi Focus tells you plainly how many days you missed. Your mandala still carries on to 48 days.

I travel a lot. How can Yogi Focus help me?

Ten minutes fits into any day, wherever you are. Take your same things with you, such as your mat or your shawl: they bring the routine into a hotel room. Choose the spot that feels closest to your usual one.

If a journey moves your hour, just start when you can. If you miss a day, nothing resets. And if travel becomes your normal life, you can change the hour or the place in your routine's settings.

Why can I only start with one routine?

One routine is something you can keep. Five is a plan that falls apart in the second week. When your first mandala of 48 days is complete, Yogi Focus will invite you to add another.

Why ten minutes?

It is short enough to fit into any day and long enough to matter, so it is where Yogi Focus suggests you begin. If your practice needs longer, you can choose 20, 30 or 45 minutes, or an hour.

In the first week Yogi Focus will even ask you to stop when your time is up, when you would like more, so you finish fresh and want to come back tomorrow.

Why can't I see my progress straight away?

Counting days turns a calm practice into a score to protect. For the first 30 days Yogi Focus keeps the count out of sight, so every day can simply be today. Your petals are still opening; on day 30 you get to see them.

What is the boring middle?

Somewhere around day seven the novelty wears off, and it often stays dull until about day 27. It is normal and it passes. In those weeks Yogi Focus has one message for you: just start.

Do I need an Apple Watch?
