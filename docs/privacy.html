Yogi Focus

Privacy

Your routine is yours. Yogi Focus keeps it on your devices and in your own iCloud, and nowhere else.

No account. No tracking. No ads. Nothing is sent to us.

What Yogi Focus keeps

To remind you and to grow your lotus, the app keeps:

  • your routines: what they are, their hour, length, place and the things you use, and whether the chime is on or the routine is paused;
  • the days you practised, and for each one how it felt (scattered, steady or absorbed) and any note you wrote;
  • where you are in your mandala, so the lotus can be shown from day 30.

Where it is kept

On your iPhone, shared with the Yogi Focus widget and your Apple Watch so they can show today's routine and let you tap Done.

If you use iCloud, the same information is also kept in the private part of your own iCloud account, so it is there when you set up a new iPhone or Apple Watch. Apple stores it for you; we cannot see it. You can turn this off in Settings, under your name, then iCloud.

What Yogi Focus does not do

  • It does not have a server, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or health data.

Reminders

The nudges before and at your hour are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are only sent if you allow notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.

Removing your data

Removing a routine in the app removes its days and notes from your devices and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is on that device; to remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings, then your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Yogi Focus.
