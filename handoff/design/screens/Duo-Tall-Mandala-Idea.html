One mandala is 48 days.

Yogis hold that a practice kept for one mandala is likely to stay for life. This is how those 48 days tend to go.

Willpower alone
A burst of effort, a crash in the boring middle, and then interest quietly dies.
With Yogi Focus
A gentle ten-minute start, a shallow dip, a nudge to just start, and by the end it runs on its own.
