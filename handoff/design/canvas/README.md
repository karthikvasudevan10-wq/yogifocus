# Design canvas

The source files of the Yogi Focus design canvas on claude.ai ("Yogi — one day at a time"), as of 19 November 2026: 55 artboards plus `canvas.json`, which places them on the canvas.

Each `.dc.html` file is one artboard. They are written for the claude.ai design canvas and load its `support.js`, so they do not render on their own in a browser. To look at a screen outside the canvas, use the matching standalone page in `../screens/` (where one exists) or its still image in `../previews/`.

New here since the handoff was first written:

- `App-Icon-V2.dc.html`: the v2 icon options (A to F). F was chosen.
- `Duo-*.dc.html`: the iPhone Duo screens.
- `*-Dark.dc.html`: the dark appearance.
