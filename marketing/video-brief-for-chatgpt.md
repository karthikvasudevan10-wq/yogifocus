## Two different videos: read this first

1. **App Store app preview.** The video that plays on the App Store page. Apple's rule (App Review Guideline 2.3.4): previews may only use video screen captures of the app itself, with optional narration and text overlays. A generated or illustrated video cannot be used here. This one has to be recorded from the finished app; the storyboard in section 9 says what to record.
2. **Promotional video.** For the website, social media, launch posts and ads. This can be animated or generated, using the app's own look. **This is the one to make with ChatGPT.**

## 1. The product

**Yogi Focus** is a free app for iPhone, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch. It helps people keep one small routine, one day at a time, using a method yogis in India have used for centuries to master any practice: meditation, yoga or any other skill.

It is made for anyone who finds it hard to focus, keep a routine or form a habit.

**One line:** One small routine. One day at a time.

**Promise:** No streaks, no guilt, just today.

## 2. The method (the story the video tells)

1. **Only today.** "For the rest of your life" is too heavy to carry. Each morning a yogi asks one question: this is a new day, how will I keep my practice today?
2. **Same time, same place, same things.** The same hour, the same spot, the same mat and clothes. When nothing around the routine changes, there is nothing to decide and nothing to remember.
3. **One mandala is 48 days.** A practice kept for one mandala is believed to show real benefits and to stay for life.

**The boring middle.** Around day 7 the novelty wears off. Willpower alone crashes here and interest dies. With Yogi Focus the dip is shallow, because the app's one message in those weeks is: just start. By days 28 to 48 the routine runs on its own.

## 3. How the app works

- Choose **one** routine, at least ten minutes. Second routines come only after the first 48 days.
- Give it an hour, a place and its things. For example: each day at 7:00 am, on the balcony, with a yoga mat and a grey shawl, meditate for ten minutes.
- A **Home Screen widget** shows today's routine. At the hour it turns yellow and says "It's time", with a Done button.
- **Apple Watch** gives a gentle tap 20 minutes before and at the hour, then asks how it felt.
- **One kind question** after practising: Scattered, Steady or Absorbed. Every answer gets a kind reply, such as "Steady is exactly how a practice grows."
- **"That's today, kept."** Then only tomorrow.
- **A 48-petal lotus**: one petal per day. Hidden for the first 30 days, then revealed. A missed day turns its petal red; nothing ever resets.
- **Day 48**: a celebration with falling petals and the Yoga Sūtra verse.

## 4. Tone

Calm, warm, encouraging, light. Bright and fresh, never clinical. Plain short sentences. It speaks to the viewer as an equal.

**Do:** celebrate small wins; say "just start"; make missing a day feel safe.

**Never:** guilt, pressure, countdowns, streak counters, "don't break the chain", sad characters, alarm sounds, before-and-after body imagery, or health or medical claims ("cures", "treats ADHD", and so on).

## 6. The characters and objects

- **The yogi.** An original flat-vector character: slim and upright, warm light-brown skin, dark-brown hair in a top bun tied with a saffron band, a gentle smile, a saffron-orange robe draped over one shoulder with the other arm bare, a string of dark-red prayer beads, a pale yellow (or white) lower robe, sitting cross-legged. Three moods: calm with eyes closed; joyful with eyes open and a broad smile; greeting with palms together (namaste). He often **floats** gently above a lotus, rising and sinking slowly with a soft shadow beneath him.
- **The lotus.** A 48-petal flower in three rings of 16 almond-shaped petals: deep green inside, mid green, leaf green outside, with a sunny yellow centre. It turns slowly. Petals open one by one, one per day practised.
- **The widget.** A rounded square card: "It's time · Meditate · Done".
- **The sun and moon.** A day passes over a green hill: the sun rises and sets, a slim crescent moon and stars come out on a dark blue sky, and a new day rises.

The screenshots in this folder show all of these. Match them; do not copy any other brand's characters.

## 9. App Store app preview: what to record (15–30 seconds)

Record on a device or simulator once the app is built. Apple's specifications: 15 to 30 seconds, up to 30 frames per second, up to three previews per device size. Accepted resolution for 6.9-inch iPhone and for iPhone Duo: 886 × 1920 portrait or 1920 × 886 landscape. Stereo audio.

1. Welcome screen: the yogi floating on the lotus (3 s).
2. "One mandala is 48 days" graph playing (4 s).
3. Choosing Meditate and setting 7:00 am, the balcony, mat and shawl (5 s).
4. The widget at "It's time", tapping Done (4 s).
5. "How did it feel?", choosing Steady, the kind reply (4 s).
6. "That's today, kept." (3 s).
7. End on the Welcome screen or the lotus (2 s).

Text overlays may use the key lines in section 7.
